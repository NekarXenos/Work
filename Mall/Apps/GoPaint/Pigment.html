Find your flow.

Watercolour spreads and blends on wet paper. More wetness makes softer edges and lively blooms. Let one colour meet another while the paint is wet.

Oil paint builds raised strokes. Increase thickness to catch the light; lower bristle density to leave more visible tracks. Splatter adds scattered droplets around your stroke.

Dry paint sets the current layer before you paint over it. Automatic rendering adjusts to your device; “After each stroke” keeps drawing responsive on slower devices.
