Drop ZX Spectrum tapes and snapshots here: .tap, .tzx, .z80 or .sna.

They show up in the "Demos and tapes" menu at any NX Dev desk. Real Spectrum
software also needs Sinclair's ROM, which does not ship with the plaza - see
Apps/Spectrum/PUT_48ROM_HERE.txt.

You can also just drag a file onto the page while you are sitting at a desk.
